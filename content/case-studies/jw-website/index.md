# Designing a responsive commerce store for premium pet food

I partnered with product management, engineering, and design leads to rebuild the ecommerce platform. We focused on restructuring the checkout funnel to remove friction points, accelerate completion times, and adapt responsively across all viewports.

## Role & scope

- **Client:** James Wellbeloved
- **Platform:** Web (ecommerce)
- **Role:** UX Designer
- **Stack:** Photoshop, Illustrator, Framer, InVision
- **Timeline:** 12 weeks
- **Squad:** Cross-functional
- **Year:** 2016

## Business challenge

The James Wellbeloved website was accessible only on desktop, offered no visible USPs, and lacked essential product nutritional information, resulting in a counterintuitive user experience. Before the redesign, this led to a high overall bounce rate of 74% (rising to 88% on mobile due to a fixed desktop viewport), a low conversion rate of 1.1%, and a 90-day customer retention rate of just 14% (those who placed a second order). With over 58% of total traffic arriving on mobile devices, the legacy platform created substantial friction in customer acquisition and restricted growth.

## Outcomes

### Fast forward 6 months

6 months after the website’s launch, I compiled quantitative data from Google Analytics to assess initial post-launch performance, validate key UX decisions, and identify areas for future iteration.

Here are the core data insights accumulated across desktop and mobile:

#### Key data insights (6-month post-launch)
- **-32% cart abandonment:** Dropped from 78% to 53% by removing checkout friction and responsive bottlenecks.
- **-73% bounce rate:** Reduced from 74% to 20% through responsive design and fresh visual branding.
- **+27% conversion rate:** Grew from 1.1% to 1.4%, significantly lowering purchase effort across all devices.
- **+68% unique visitors:** Expanded monthly visits from 23.5k to 39.5k as mobile search accessibility improved.
- **+100% customer retention:** Doubled 90-day repeat purchases from 14% to 28% via clearer navigation and re-ordering flows.

### Retrospective

- **Grounded in Data First:** Prioritising existing user data and audit insights before designing gave the project immediate clarity and direction.
- **Pragmatic Methodology:** Rather than getting bogged down in complex UX frameworks, I focused on a lean, logical design approach to surface actionable insights quickly.
- **Developer Alignment:** Continuous collaboration with engineering ensured all proposed UX solutions were technically feasible within our 3-month timeline.
- **Future Opportunity:** Given more time, conducting formal usability testing post-launch would have provided deeper qualitative insight into long-term user friction and behaviour.
