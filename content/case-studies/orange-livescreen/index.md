# Increasing engagement through a news app redesign

I collaborated with stakeholders, product managers and developers to redesign the Livescreen news app. By leveraging the trustworthy Orange brand, the goal was to increase user engagement and increase revenue.

## Role & scope

- **Client:** Orange Telecom
- **Platform:** Android
- **Role:** UX Designer
- **Stack:** Sketch, Photoshop, Illustrator, Miro, InVision
- **Timeline:** 6 months
- **Squad:** Cross-functional
- **Year:** 2019

![Orange Livescreen app redesign hero](/images/ls-hero-banner.jpg)

## Executive summary

Livescreen served as a primary news and engagement channel to promote Orange ecosystem apps, partner services, and subscriptions across five European markets. However, legacy navigation structures caused active user engagement to plummet, rendering promotional spaces ineffective. To reverse this trend, I restructured the app around customisable frontend content.

- Empowered reader customisation by designing modular topic selection and real-time article translation tools.
- Built localised publishing controls enabling regional managers across 5 European markets to deploy targeted offers.
- **Increased user engagement by 32% while driving 17% promotional revenue growth** through tailored content surfacing at the right time.

## Business challenge

I led the UX redesign of Orange's Livescreen news app across five European markets, France, Belgium, Spain, Poland, and Romania, to stop engagement from dropping and restore promotional conversion.

- **Identified primary commercial drop-off:** Legacy navigation patterns caused active user retention to drop, lowering visibility for ecosystem offers and partner services.
- **Restructured content discovery:** Redesigned the core news interface to reduce browsing friction and increase article interaction rates.
- **Restored revenue channels:** Re-engaged active users to increase ecosystem app downloads, gift redemptions, and paid service sign-ups.

### Affinity mapping the findings

I facilitated a remote workshop to run a collaborative affinity mapping session. Working with the cross-functional team, I structured direct user feedback into four core categories: **Behaviours**, **Pains**, **Motivations**, and **Feature requests.** Grouping these observations removed ambiguity around user friction points, establishing a clear baseline to define product requirements and align our project scope.

### Key user test findings

Overall, the Livescreen app was very well received. Users really liked the UI and intuitively discovered features such as adding a topic via the + icon, adding and removing stories. Here are the remaining commonalities found:

- **23/25 intuitive navigation:** Found the bottom navigation bar a clear and intuitive way to navigate the app.
- **24/25 regional news demand:** Expected regional news coverage to be readily available within the core experience.
- **22/25 saving articles:** Saving articles was identified as an essential feature for long-term engagement.
- **18/25 video relevance gap:** Felt the video section layout lacked content variety and relevant recommendations.
- **23/25 content tailoring:** Valued topic customisation, requesting an initial onboarding tooltip to guide setup.
- **22/25 intrusive ad placement:** Found current ads intrusive and preferred placement positioned near the bottom.
- **21/25 churn risk:** Indicated they would uninstall the app if exposed to an excessive volume of ads.
- **25/25 ad relevance:** Stated that all displayed advertisements must directly align with their personal interests.
- **24/25 full localisation:** Requested language translation options for both article content and the overall app UI.

## Outcomes

The redesigned Livescreen application successfully reduced user churn and increased engagement. By aligning frontend customisation with regional backend controls, we achieved tangible business impact:

- **Increased user engagement by 32%** through intuitive navigation and modular topic selection.
- **Drove 17% promotional revenue growth** via targeted offers deployed by regional managers across five European markets.

### Retrospective

When delivering a complex multi-region product across diverse European markets, building strong cross-functional alignment from day one is critical. Balancing regional stakeholder requirements with distinct cultural user needs required robust early collaboration to ensure on-time delivery without compromising local needs and trends.
